Lumine

Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Lumine is an Android app for trying on jewelry in augmented reality, built as a university thesis project. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

The camera is only used for the live try-on. Camera images are processed on your phone to find your hand and face. They are never recorded, saved or sent to our servers.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password. Your password travels over an encrypted connection and is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider. We never store it in readable form.
  • Profile details you choose to add: username, first and last name, mobile number and profile photo.
  • Favorites: the items you save.
  • Ratings: the star ratings and optional comments you give after a try-on.
  • Technical logs: when the app talks to our server, the server records the time, the request and your IP address. We use these to keep the service working and secure, for example to limit repeated sign-in attempts.

Lumine has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and does not sell or share your data for advertising.

How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in, including emailing you verification and password-reset codes.
  • To show your profile and favorites.
  • To show average ratings per item to the store's administrator.
  • To keep the service running and secure.

Who can see it

  • You can see and edit your profile in the app.
  • The store's administrator can see account emails, profile details and favorites in the admin panel, to manage the store and its users. Ratings are shown to them as averages per item.
  • Service providers store and process data for us: Supabase (database, sign-in, photo storage and code emails) and Akamai Cloud, also known as Linode (hosts our server). They process it only to provide these services.

Profile photos are stored at an unlisted web address so the app can display them. All data travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: open your Profile and tap Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent, and removes:

  • your account and email address
  • your profile details and profile photo
  • your favorites

Your ratings and their comments stay in the store's rating totals, but are no longer linked to you or your account. Technical logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.

No longer have the app? Follow the steps on the account deletion page.

Children

Lumine is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
