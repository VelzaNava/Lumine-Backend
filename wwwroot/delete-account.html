Delete your Lumine account

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Lumine and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete account again to confirm.

Without the app

Email lumine.jrp@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We will delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Your profile details: username, name, mobile number and profile photo
  • Your saved favorites

What is kept

Star ratings and their comments stay in the store's rating totals, but are no longer linked to you or your account. Server security logs are kept only as long as needed, then deleted.

More details are in the privacy policy.
